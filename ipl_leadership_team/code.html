Uncompromising Trust, Honorary Stewardship & Zero Leakage

The Indian Penpals' League (IPL) operates under strict provisions of the Bombay Public Trusts Act, 1950. No trustee or coordinator receives any administrative remuneration, per-diem allowances, or executive perks. All donations collected are applied directly toward certified humanitarian relief work and education assistance programs.
