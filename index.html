About Indian Penpals' League

A registered social welfare trust — 30 years of unbroken friendship, correspondence, and community service.

Our Story

The INDIAN PENPALS' LEAGUE (IPL) is a confederation of groups of friends in India and abroad. IPL had its inception in Mumbai on March 12, 1995, formed by like-minded pen-pals coming together to uphold three basic principles of Love, Friendship and Humanity, overcoming the barriers of caste, color, creed, religion, language and geographical bounds separating humankind.

Founded by M. Karun — a passionate believer in the power of handwritten correspondence — the League has grown from a small circle of pen-pals into a nation-wide confederation spanning 14 Indian states, with members abroad, bound not by geography but by the timeless art of letter writing.

History & Legacy of IPL

Three decades of friendship, correspondence, and humanitarian service across India.

1995

Founding — March 12, 1995

Indian Penpals' League founded in Mumbai, Chembur by M. Karun and a group of like-minded pen-pals. Registration #F23778 obtained. First Friends Day celebrated with 50 founding members.

2000

National Expansion

IPL chapters expanded to Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, and Kerala. First blood donation drive organized with 100+ units collected. Membership reached 300+ active correspondents.

2005

Silver Decade

10-year anniversary celebrated with a grand national convention. Student sponsorship programme launched sponsoring 50+ students. IPL bulletin began bilingual (Tamil & English) publication.

2010

Humanitarian Wings Formalized

Four formal humanitarian wings established: Blood Donor Registry, Student Aid Programme, Hospice Letter-Writing Wing, and Disaster Relief Coordination. 80G certification obtained.

2015

Pearl Anniversary

20th anniversary celebrated with delegates from 14 states. Commemorative souvenir book published. Inner Wheel Club collaboration formalized for rural student outreach in Tiruvannamalai.

2020

Resilience Through Pandemic

IPL continued correspondence activities digitally during COVID-19. Relief packages and support provided to vulnerable members. Letter-writing kept the spirit of friendship alive.

2025

Pearl Jubilee — 30th Anniversary

Historic 30th year Pearl Jubilee celebration. Grand national convention with 500+ delegates. Pearl Jubilee commemorative hardbound book launched documenting 30 years of fellowship, philanthropy, and friendship.

Pearl Jubilee Message — 2025

30 Years of Friendship — A Personal Reflection

When I penned my first letter as a pen-pal in the early 1990s, I never imagined it would one day become the seed of a movement. That modest exchange of thoughts — handwritten, heartfelt — blossomed into the Indian Penpals' League, an organization I have had the privilege to serve as Founder-President for three decades.

Thirty years ago, we began with a simple belief: that friendship transcends borders, languages, castes, and creeds. We believed that a handwritten letter carries a warmth that no digital message can replicate — it carries the writer's breath, their patience, their care. And through tens of thousands of such letters, we built something extraordinary: a confederation of friends.

"Let us breathe friendship! Let us love our friends! Let us sow love, friendship, and humanity! Let us nurture and continue tirelessly, the journey of friendship..."

Today, as we celebrate our Pearl Jubilee, I look back with immense gratitude — to every member who wrote a letter, attended a meet, donated blood, sponsored a student, or simply stayed faithful to our principles of Love, Friendship and Humanity. You are IPL.

Looking ahead, I invite the next generation to join us in keeping the art of correspondence alive. Write a letter. Make a friend. Change a life.

With enduring friendship, M. Karun

Annual Friends Day Message — 2024

Friends Day: Celebrating the Bond That Never Fades

As we gather once again for our Annual Friends Day Convention, I am reminded of why we started this journey. Friendship is not merely a sentiment — it is a discipline, a commitment, a daily choice to remain connected despite distance, time, and the distractions of modern life.

To every delegate traveling from across India to be with us today — thank you. Your presence is a testament to the enduring power of pen-pal friendship. Let this day remind us: a letter written is a bridge built. Keep writing.

With warmth, M. Karun

What is Friends Day?

Friends Day (நட்பு தினம்) is IPL's flagship annual national convention — a grand gathering of pen-pals who became lifelong friends through the art of letter writing.

Each year, delegates from 14 Indian states travel to celebrate friendship, exchange letters, honor outstanding correspondents, and launch the year's commemorative envelope and souvenir bulletin.
