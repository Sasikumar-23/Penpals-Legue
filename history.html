history 30 Years of Unbroken Commitment (1995–2025)

Milestones in the Journey of Friendship

From a humble group of pen-pals exchanging inland letters in Chembur to an accredited pan-India humanitarian public trust with over 120,000 correspondence links and 300+ medical relief drives.

01
Foundation

March 12, 1995 — Inception in Chembur, Mumbai

Year 1995

Initiated by Founder President M. Karun alongside an energetic group of correspondents in Chembur, Mumbai. The founding charter declared pen-pal networks as a viable vehicle to break communal, linguistic, and regional barriers across India through Love, Friendship, and Humanity.

Key Milestone: First 500 inland letters exchanged between youth in Maharashtra and Tamil Nadu.
02
Interstate Expansion

1998 — Expansion to Southern & Western States

Year 1998

Chapter cells were established in Chennai, Madurai, Tiruchirappalli, Bengaluru, and Pune. IPL launched its quarterly postal bulletin connecting literary clubs, students, and philatelists across 8 Indian languages.

03
Statutory Accreditation

2000 — Official Public Trust Registration #F23778

Trust Reg #F23778

Formally registered under the Maharashtra Public Trust Act before the Mumbai Charity Commissioner. The Trust Deed codified honorary governance with zero administrative leakage and direct welfare accountability.

04
Disaster Relief

2004–2005 — Tsunami Disaster Relief Expeditions

Year 2004–2005

Following the 2004 Indian Ocean Tsunami, IPL mobilized members across Maharashtra to dispatch 14 metric tonnes of clothing, dry rations, and medical emergency kits to coastal fishing hamlets in Nagapattinam and Cuddalore.

05
Education Wing

2010 — Launch of Annual Notebook & School Kits Drive

Year 2010

Formal launch of the Student Aid Wing providing comprehensive stationery sets, uniforms, and school bags to municipal school pupils across Chembur, Govandi, and rural Maharashtra schools.

06
Pearl Jubilee

2025 — 30th Year Commemorative Jubilee & Modern Redesign

30-Year Jubilee

Celebration of three continuous decades of uninterrupted service, release of the 30th Year Commemorative Book, and the rollout of the modern digital platform to connect the next generation of correspondents.
