Active Operations

Five Wings of Selfless Relief

Conducted continuously through voluntary member subscriptions and local collaborations.

Wing 01 • Education

Student Aid & Annual Notebook Drives

Every academic year, IPL delivers comprehensive school supply kits—notebooks, stationery sets, uniforms, and school bags—to underprivileged pupils across Chembur, Govandi, and deep rural schools in Maharashtra and Tamil Nadu.

Wing 02 • Healthcare

Health & Medical Camps

Regular voluntary blood donation drives in partnership with state blood banks, paired with specialized eye care, free screenings, and cataract surgeries for elders.

Emergency Donor Directory: 1,400+ member penfriends ready on 2-hour alert across Mumbai Metropolitan Region.

Wing 03 • Geriatric

Elderly Fellowship & Outreach

Combating urban loneliness among seniors through listening circles, home wellness visits, and monthly nutritious dry ration grocery kits.
