“Let Us Breathe Friendship, Love Our Friends, and Sow Humanity”

“When we founded the Indian Penpals' League on March 12, 1995, in Mumbai, we envisioned a brotherhood where ink on paper could melt physical borders, caste barriers, and language divides.
