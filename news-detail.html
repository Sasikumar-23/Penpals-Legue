Fellowship Meet Aug 25, 2024 Chembur, Mumbai

???????? ???? ????????? ????????? (Cordial Fellowship Gathering)

person Report filed by Secretariat Desk • Pres. M. Karun presiding

A cordial gathering of dedicated pen-pals and social volunteers convened at Chembur, Mumbai under the leadership of Founder President M. Karun. Delegates from Chennai, Madurai, Pune, and suburban Mumbai attended the meet to review ongoing welfare drives and plan the upcoming academic year's student stationery distribution.

The session featured the distribution of quarterly handwritten letter exchange diaries, member felicitations, and an address reaffirming the League's 30-year commitment to Love, Friendship, and Humanity without distinction of caste, religion, or linguistic barriers.

Key Resolutions & Announcements

  • check_circle Target set for 15,000 student notebook kits across 50 municipal schools.
  • check_circle Upcoming quarterly blood donation drive scheduled in Chembur.
