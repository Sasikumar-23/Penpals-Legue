Friends Day Annual National Convention & Letter Exchange

Bringing together correspondents, philanthropists, youth writers, and philatelists from 14 Indian states to celebrate 30 years of Love, Friendship & Humanity.

calendar_month Sunday, First Week of August location_on Chembur Convention Auditorium, Mumbai
Program Itinerary

Official Convention Schedule

09:30 AM – 10:30 AM Inaugural

Welcome Proclamation & Lamp Lighting

Inauguration by Founder President M. Karun alongside visiting trustees from Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, and Maharashtra.

10:45 AM – 12:30 PM Philately & Scribes

Postal Heritage Exhibition & Letter Exchange

Release of the Commemorative Postal First-Day Cover, handwritten diary distributions, and philatelic stamp showcase.

01:30 PM – 03:30 PM Social Impact

Kalvi Karangal Student Aid & Honor Conclave

Distribution of 500+ school kits to municipal students and felicitation of veteran penfriends with 25+ years of active correspondence.
