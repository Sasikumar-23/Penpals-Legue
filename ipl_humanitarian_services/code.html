Active Operations

Five Pillars of Selfless Relief

Conducted continuously through voluntary member subscriptions, local ward collaborations, and corporate CSR co-sponsors.

Wing 01 • Education
Annual Reach

12,500+ Notebooks Distributed

school

Student Aid & Annual Notebook Drives

Every academic year, IPL delivers comprehensive school supply kits—durable notebooks, geometric stationery kits, school uniforms, and bag sets—to underprivileged pupils across Chembur, Govandi, and deep rural schools in Maharashtra and Tamil Nadu. We also facilitate secondary school fee stipends for deserving single-parent students.

Wing 02 • Healthcare
Clinical Camps

185+ Blood & Eye Camps

Health & Medical Camps

Regular voluntary blood donation drives in partnership with state blood banks, paired with specialized eye care. We provide free screening, prescription spectacles, cataract surgeries, and basic geriatric diabetes medication in Chembur and surrounding suburban hamlets.

vital_signs

Emergency Donor Directory: 1,400+ member penfriends ready on 2-hour alert across Mumbai Metropolitan Region.

Wing 03 • Geriatric Care

240+ Elders Visited

Elderly Fellowship & Outreach

Combating urban loneliness among retired citizens through dedicated listening circles, bi-weekly physical home wellness visits, assistance with hospital appointments, and monthly nutritious dry ration kits for economically fragile elders.

Wing 04 • Penfriendship Core

18,000+ Letters Delivered

Prison & Hospice Letter Writing

Rooted in our 1995 penfriend heritage. Certified, vetted penpals write empathetic letters of hope, moral strength, and rehabilitation to institutionalized citizens, correctional facility inmates, and palliative cancer patients across certified hospices.

Wing 05 • Rapid Action

Interstate Relief Fleet

Disaster Relief & Emergency Aid

Rapid on-the-ground mobilization during natural crises. From the 2004 Indian Ocean Tsunami, Maharashtra floods, to pandemic emergency oxygen concierges, our nationwide network of pen-pal coordinators dispatches direct grain kits, blankets, and purification tablets.
