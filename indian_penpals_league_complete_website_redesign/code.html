Official Mission

Welcome to
INDIAN PENPALS' LEAGUE

The INDIAN PENPALS' LEAGUE (IPL) is a confederation of groups of friends in India and abroad. IPL had its inception in Mumbai on March 12, 1995, formed by like-minded pen-pals coming together to uphold three basic principles of Love, Friendship and Humanity, overcoming the barriers of caste, color, creed, religion, language and geographical bounds separating humankind.

30 Years of Unbroken Commitment (1995–2025)

Milestones in the Journey of Friendship

history_edu Registered under Mumbai Public Trust Act
01
March 12, 1995 Inception in Mumbai

The Spark of Like-Minded Pen-Pals

Initiated by M. Karun alongside an energetic group of correspondents in Chembur, Mumbai. The founding charter declared pen-pal networks as a viable vehicle to break communal, linguistic, and regional barriers across India.

02
2000 – 2008 Interstate Expansion

Penpal Meets & National Conventions

Active chapters took shape across Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, and Delhi. Annual 'Friends Day' gatherings provided platforms for face-to-face greetings between correspondents who had written each other for years.

03
Trust & 80G Grant Institutional Solidification

Formal Charity Registration #F23778

80G Status

Transformed into an officially registered Social Welfare Trust under the Office of the Charity Commissioner, Mumbai. All donors receive statutory tax exemptions under Section 80G of the Indian Income Tax Act.

04
2025 – 2026 Pearl Jubilee Era

30th Year Pearl Jubilee & Modern Reach

Celebrating three decades of persistent volunteerism with upgraded digital correspondence systems, expanded educational sponsorship funds, and broader outreach for destitute senior citizens.
