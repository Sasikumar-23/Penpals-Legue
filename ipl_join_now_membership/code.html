Common Queries

Frequently Asked Questions

Essential details on safety, postal etiquette, and our League guidelines.

shield

How does IPL ensure safety and address privacy?

Every applicant's identity is reviewed by the League Trust before approval. Member addresses are shared strictly with verified reciprocal penfriends based on shared language and hobby interests. Our Trust enforces a strict Code of Honor: addresses are never published openly on the internet or traded with commercial vendors.

payments

Is there any hidden fee or annual renewal charge?

No. Enrollment in the Indian Penpals' League is completely free of cost as per our registered social welfare charter (Trust Reg #F23778). Members bear only their own postal stamp costs for the letters they choose to post.

history_edu

What is the recommended postal correspondence etiquette?

Members are expected to acknowledge received letters within 2 to 3 weeks. Always maintain polite, warm, and uplifting dialogue. Avoid contentious sectarian debates or unsolicited financial requests. Sincerity, legible handwriting, and mutual cultural curiosity are our core tenets.

diversity_3

Can I start or join an IPL Chapter in my local town or city?

Yes. Once registered, active members may coordinate with our Mumbai headquarters to organize localized letter-writing circles, school postal awareness drives, or collaborate on IPL blood donation and environmental relief camps.

support_agent

Need assistance with your registration?

Our Mumbai Trust office will be happy to guide you via phone or postal correspondence.
