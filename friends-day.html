Friends Day

நட்பு தினம்

Every year since 1995, the Indian Penpals' League gathers pen-pals, trustees, and friends from across India for its most celebrated event — Friends Day. A day of joy, letters, awards, and renewed friendship pledges.

What is Friends Day?

Friends Day (நட்பு தினம்) is the Indian Penpals' League's flagship annual national convention — a grand gathering of pen-pals who became lifelong friends through the art of letter writing.

Each year, delegates from 14 Indian states travel to the venue to celebrate friendship, exchange letters, honor outstanding correspondents with penfriend awards, and launch the year's commemorative envelope and souvenir bulletin. A youth literacy pledge and student sponsorship announcements are also made.

The event is personally organized and chaired by Founder-President M. Karun and is the most important gathering in the IPL calendar.

What Happens at Friends Day?

Every Friends Day celebration includes these cherished traditions

mail

Letter Exchange Ceremony

Members exchange handwritten letters as a symbolic renewal of the pen-pal tradition — the heart of IPL's mission.

emoji_events

Penfriend Awards

Outstanding correspondents are felicitated for their dedication to letter writing, community service, and promoting friendship across borders.

local_post_office

Commemorative Envelope Release

A specially designed Friends Day commemorative envelope is released each year and dispatched to all IPL members across India.

school

Youth Literacy Drive

New student members are inducted and a fresh literacy pledge is taken, with announcements of student sponsorships for the coming year.

auto_stories

Souvenir Bulletin

A bilingual (Tamil & English) souvenir bulletin containing member registries, poems, photographs, and social welfare reports is released.

volunteer_activism

Humanitarian Announcements

New blood donation targets, disaster relief pledges, and charitable undertakings for the year are formally announced at the convention.
