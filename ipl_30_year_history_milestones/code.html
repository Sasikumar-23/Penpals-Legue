Historical Chronicle

Epochs of Fellowship & Public Good

Trace the thirty-year evolution from handwritten inland letters to recognized statewide disaster relief and humanitarian education.

March 12, 1995

League Inception at Chembur

Under the primary vision of Founder M. Karun, thirty-four dedicated postal correspondents gathered in Mumbai to sign the charter establishing Indian Penpals' League. The mission: foster interstate cultural brotherhood using postal letters, stamps, and genuine human warmth.

2000 – 2008

National Conventions & State Chapters

Penpals who had communicated solely by mail met in person during landmark annual 'Friends Day' conventions. Regional chapters blossomed across Karnataka, Delhi, and rural districts of Tamil Nadu, creating an authentic cross-border social network before the rise of the digital internet.

groups Over 3,500 active postal correspondents
June 2012

Public Welfare Trust Reg #F23778

Transitioning from an informal hobby league to an institutional non-profit body, IPL achieved legal registration under the Charity Commissioner of Greater Mumbai. The newly adopted trust deed codified voluntary blood donation camps, free medicine relief, and community aid drives.

2018

80G Exemption & Educational Drives

Recognition by the Indian Income Tax Department with 80G tax-exempt charitable status allowed IPL to scale philanthropic partnerships. Initiated annual rural student sponsorships, free uniform distribution, and specialized senior citizen healthcare outreach camps in suburban Mumbai.

2024 – 2026

Pearl Jubilee (30 Years) & Digital Era

Commemorating 30 continuous years of voluntary brotherhood. The league inaugurated the digital postal correspondence archive, youth global penpal exchanges, and expanded emergency medical relief wings across disaster-affected zones in peninsular India.
